Enrolling faces

Press the ✗ in the Face column. That student turns amber and is pushed to every terminal with their device id showing. Send them to a machine and enrol the face against that id — the moment the terminal reports back, the row ticks by itself. You can leave this page open on a phone while you work through a class.

A student with no device id gets one automatically when you press ✗, so there is nothing to set up first. Card numbers save as you type — scan straight into the RFID box and it is stored.

Take this student off the roll

Their attendance history is kept, so past reports stay correct. They stop appearing on registers and stop receiving SMS.
