How it works

The sample workbook already knows your school: the class, shift, version and group columns come with dropdowns of what exists today, and the identifier and phone columns are locked to text so 01712345678 keeps its leading zero.

Anything you type that does not exist yet is created — a new class, a new shift, a new section, a new group. You do not need to set up the structure first. After the import you get a list of exactly what was created, so a typo like “Clas Six” is obvious before it spreads through the roll.
