How a punch becomes attendance

When someone verifies on a terminal, the server looks up their device user id, finds the slots their class is in today, and picks the one whose window contains that moment. Before the late cut-off it is present; after it, late. Anyone with no punch by the time the window closes is marked absent by the scheduled job.
